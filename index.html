Open Trail Planner

Offline Drakensberg trail map and trip planner

Open Trail Planner is an offline topographic map and multi-day trip planner for hiking in the Drakensberg, South Africa and Lesotho. All map data is built into the app, so it works in the mountains without a signal.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas: email qss@qtx.co.za. Tell us which phone you use and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Which area does the map cover?

The KwaZulu-Natal Drakensberg escarpment and the Lesotho border area, from the Sentinel and Royal Natal in the north to the southern Berg. Outside this area the map is blank.

Do I need a signal or data?

No. The map, trails, elevation and your GPS position all work offline.

My position doesn't show on the map.

Allow location access for Open Trail Planner in your phone's settings, and make sure you're inside the Drakensberg map area. Under open sky the first GPS fix can take a minute.

How do I get a route onto my Garmin watch?

Open the trip's Overview and tap Export → GPX. You get one course per day. Import it at connect.garmin.com (Training & Planning → Courses → Import) and send it to the watch, or copy the .gpx into the watch's GARMIN/NewFiles folder over USB.

How do I share a trip with friends?

In the Overview, tap Export → Trail plan and send the .trpl file. They open it with Open Trail Planner and get the whole plan: days, camps, stops and notes.

A trail is missing or wrong.

The trails come from OpenStreetMap, which anyone can improve. Fixes made there appear in a later version of the app. You can also email us the details.

Is it safe to rely on the app alone?

No. OpenStreetMap shows what volunteers have mapped, and some trails may be missing or out of date. Always carry a paper map, check conditions and register with the park before you hike.

Privacy

The app collects no data. No accounts, tracking or ads, and your location never leaves your phone. Read the full privacy policy.

Open Trail Planner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Open Trail Planner is published by QTX. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short, it collects none.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your phone's GPS position to show where you are on the map. It reads your position only while the app is open, and the position stays on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Routes and settings

Routes you plan and settings you change (such as pace profiles) are stored only on your device. If you export a GPX file, you choose where it goes using your phone's share sheet.

No accounts, tracking or ads

The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party tracking. All map data is built into the app, so it does not need an internet connection to work.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
